## What `v(S)` is

per-query-input log probability of the target continuation (248 = 8 queries x 31 inputs); higher is better

- **Evidence type:** retraining
- **Learner:** 8-layer width-256 step-supervised causal GPT, trained from scratch for 1000 steps per coalition; absent role embeddings tied to the identity embedding after every optimizer update
- **Cost of one coalition:** median 47.8 GPU-seconds per coalition (elapsed_seconds over all 256 cells)
- **Outputs:** 248 query inputs, named query:<q>:input:<i>
- **Secondary channel:** per-query-input exact-match correctness (0/1)
- **Licence:** derived from synthetic modular-arithmetic data generated by this project; no third-party data licence applies

## Measured cells

- coalition sizes 0–8; 256 distinct coalitions of the 256 possible
- splits: `fit` 187, `gate` 15, `target` 54
- learner seeds: 31 (256 cells)
- same-seed noise replicates: 0
- **no retraining-noise measurement**: one seed and no replicates, so the noise ceiling for this cube is unknown

## Caveats

- one learner seed (31); no retraining-noise replicate, so the noise ceiling for this cube is unknown
- the 15 gate cells and the 54 target cells were run under the same protocol as the 187 fit cells, but the fit/target split was frozen before outcomes were seen; keep the split when reproducing a surrogate comparison
- two providers serve each role by construction, so the game is redundant by design and single-player attributions are small

## Conventions

- bit convention: masks[n] has bit i (value 2**i) set iff player i is in the coalition; player 0 is the least significant bit
