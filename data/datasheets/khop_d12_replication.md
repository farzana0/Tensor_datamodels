## What `v(S)` is

per-query-input log probability of the target continuation (10 queries x 31 inputs)

- **Evidence type:** retraining
- **Learner:** 8-layer width-256 step-supervised causal GPT, 1000 steps, trained from scratch (the 15_ learner)
- **Cost of one coalition:** median 47.8 GPU-seconds (elapsed_seconds)
- **Outputs:** 310 query inputs, named query:<q>:input:<i>
- **Secondary channel:** per-query-input exact-match correctness (0/1)
- **Licence:** synthetic modular-relation data generated by this project

## Exact ground truth

Not available: this cube is sampled, so `cubes.py` raises `IncompleteCube` for Shapley, Banzhaf, LOO and interaction queries. Fit a surrogate on `cube.observed(output)` instead.

## Caveats

- SAMPLED: 4096 coalitions exist; seeds 43/59 reuse the development run's frozen masks byte-for-byte, plus LOO cells and held-out size-4/5/6 nominee cells
- every cell here ran on Quadro RTX 8000 (cell_notes); the seed-31 design cells of the development run are NOT included because 46% of them ran on V100/A6000 (they are in khop_d12_transformer); a backend switch moves a cell by SD ~0.31 nats
- size-4/5/6 evaluation cells are surrogate nominees, not a uniform sample
- leave-one-group-out cells give LOO scores identically 0 in this game by construction
- cells record the original pre-registration hash 1120ae2a although later stages ran under amended versions 

## Conventions

- bit convention: masks[n] has bit i (value 2**i) set iff player i is in the coalition; player 0 is the least significant bit
