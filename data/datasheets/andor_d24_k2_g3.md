## What `v(S)` is

per-query-input log probability of the target continuation (10 queries x 31 inputs)

- **Evidence type:** retraining
- **Learner:** 8-layer width-256 step-supervised causal GPT trained from scratch per coalition (the 15_ learner, 1000 steps), common seed per coalition
- **Cost of one coalition:** median 47.6 GPU-seconds (elapsed_seconds)
- **Outputs:** 310 query inputs, named query:<q>:input:<i>
- **Secondary channel:** per-query-input exact-match correctness (0/1)
- **Licence:** synthetic modular-relation data generated by this project

## Exact ground truth

Not available: this cube is sampled, so `cubes.py` raises `IncompleteCube` for Shapley, Banzhaf, LOO and interaction queries. Fit a surrogate on `cube.observed(output)` instead.

## Caveats

- SAMPLED: 2**24 coalitions exist; the frozen nested design (256/512/1024 training masks), 256 held-out evaluation cells and a gate/confirmation set were retrained
- the three learner seeds are near-replicates of one function (between-seed SD 0.088 nats vs 4.7 within-seed across cells); design variability is not represented
- all cells ran on Quadro RTX 8000 (see cell_notes); do not pool with other backends
- the utility is essentially an AND indicator over roles (pure step), so accuracy-style partial credit is absent by construction

## Conventions

- bit convention: masks[n] has bit i (value 2**i) set iff player i is in the coalition; player 0 is the least significant bit
