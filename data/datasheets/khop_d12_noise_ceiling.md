## What `v(S)` is

per-query-input log probability of the target continuation (10 queries x 31 inputs)

- **Evidence type:** retraining
- **Learner:** the 15_ GPT learner; arms vary one factor at a time
- **Cost of one coalition:** median 47.3 GPU-seconds (elapsed_seconds)
- **Outputs:** 310 query inputs, named query:<q>:input:<i>
- **Secondary channel:** per-query-input exact-match correctness (0/1)
- **Licence:** synthetic modular-relation data generated by this project

## Measured cells

- coalition sizes 0–12; 20 distinct coalitions of the 4096 possible
- splits: `a6000` 40, `base` 40, `data1` 40, `data2` 40, `init1` 40, `init2` 40, `rerun` 40, `v100` 40
- learner seeds: 31 (160 cells), 43 (160 cells)
- same-seed noise replicates: 280 cells repeat an already-measured coalition

## Exact ground truth

Not available: this cube is sampled, so `cubes.py` raises `IncompleteCube` for Shapley, Banzhaf, LOO and interaction queries. Fit a surrogate on `cube.observed(output)` instead.

## Caveats

- every (mask, learner seed) appears 8 times (seeds holds the arm's base learner seed 31 or 43; the perturbed init/data seeds are in cell_notes): split names the arm — base, rerun (same seeds, same GPU model, other host), init1/init2 (weight-init seed), data1/data2 (data-order seed), v100 and a6000 (GPU model); replicate > 0 marks the non-base arms
- rerun is bit-identical to base on all 40 pairs: training is deterministic at fixed seed on RTX 8000, so the rerun arm must not be pooled into a variance estimate
- this is a noise-measurement cube, not a coalition game: 20 masks only

## Conventions

- bit convention: masks[n] has bit i (value 2**i) set iff player i is in the coalition; player 0 is the least significant bit
